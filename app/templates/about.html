PassItOn – Campus Edition

"One student's unused item becomes another student's useful item."

Project Mission & Overview

College students spend thousands of rupees each semester on textbooks, scientific calculators, laboratory coats, drafting instruments, and hostel gear. At the end of the term, these items often sit idle in dorm rooms or end up in landfills. Meanwhile, junior students entering the new semester pay high retail prices for the exact same items.

PassItOn solves this campus-wide problem by providing a localized, verified student marketplace. Students buy, sell, and reuse items directly within the campus perimeter, eliminating shipping charges, middleman commissions, and fraud risks.

Architecture & Tech Stack
Backend Framework
Python 3.14 + Flask (Modular Blueprint Architecture)
Database & ORM
SQLite with SQLAlchemy (Full Foreign Keys & Relational Cascades)
Authentication & Security
Flask-Login, Werkzeug password hashing, Flask-WTF CSRF protection
QR Generation & Scanning
Python `qrcode` + Pillow, Client camera `html5-qrcode`
UI & Dashboard
Bootstrap 5, Custom CSS, Vanilla JS, Chart.js Analytics
Algorithmic Innovation
Rule-based Smart Price Suggestion (condition + age + category blend)
Academic Mini-Project Submission: All payment channels, UPI displays, and handover QR verification flows operate in a standalone demo environment suitable for offline viva evaluations and local campus hosting.
