Safe & Simple

How PassItOn Works on Campus

No shipping hassles or strangers. Transact smoothly within the campus gates.

1. Snap & List in 60s

Take a few pictures of your unused book, calculator or gear. Our Smart Price tool suggests fair prices.

2. Connect & Chat

Students in need send requests. Chat safely through our messaging system to fix a campus meeting spot.

3. Scan UPI / Cash

Pay directly via seller's UPI / GPay QR or bring exact cash during physical item inspection.

4. QR Handover

Seller scans the buyer's single-use security QR to verify handover and mark item as Sold.

Campus Handover Safety Priority

Always meet at well-lit campus landmarks like the Central Library, Cafeteria, or Department lobbies.
