Campus Community Safety Guidelines

PassItOn is designed to keep campus commerce transparent, accountable, and safe for all students.

1. Safe Campus Meetup Spots

Always arrange in-person item handovers in public, well-lit campus zones during normal daylight or operating hours.

  • Campus Central Library lobby or porch
  • Student Center / Campus Cafeteria
  • Department Academic Block foyers
  • Hostel Security Checkpoints
2. Inspect Before Final Payment

Always thoroughly test and verify physical condition of items prior to paying:

  • Calculators: test all keypads, battery compartments, and matrix/calculus functions
  • Electronics: plug into a wall outlet or power bank to check display and ports
  • Books: check for missing pages, heavy water damage, or binding issues
3. Verify with Handover QR Code

Always use the platform's QR Handover verification step.

  • The buyer receives a unique encrypted Handover QR code after payment
  • The seller scans it with their phone camera or types the security code
  • This officially completes the transaction and marks the item Sold
  • Prevents double-selling and provides an audit trail for both students
4. Report Suspicious Activity

Our campus administrators actively monitor and moderate the platform:

  • Click the "Report" button on any suspicious listing or student profile
  • Never share your email passwords, ATM PINs, or bank OTPs
  • Report students asking to meet off-campus in secluded areas
  • Campus moderators can instantly suspend non-compliant accounts
Notice any violation of campus regulations?

You can report inappropriate listings or users directly from their respective item and profile pages.
